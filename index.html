FAQ

Questions factory owners usually ask.

Can solar run an entire pure water factory?

It can power substantial factory loads when the system is correctly sized, but the right design depends on your machines, their startup requirements, operating hours and budget. In some factories, a hybrid solar + battery + generator/grid setup is the most practical approach.

Will I still need my generator?

Possibly. The goal is not always to throw away the generator; it is to stop depending on it for every hour of production. We can integrate the generator as backup for periods of unusually high demand, poor solar conditions or other contingencies.

How do you know what size system my factory needs?

We start with the factory's actual electrical loads and production schedule. That is why the assessment comes before the final recommendation.

Can the system be expanded later?

In many cases, yes—if expansion is considered during the initial design. We can recommend an architecture that leaves room for future production growth where practical.

How much does it cost?

There is no responsible one-price answer without knowing your factory load. A small production setup and a large multi-line factory require very different systems. Request an assessment and we can recommend the appropriate capacity and budget range.

What information should I send first?

Your factory location, approximate production hours, current generator size, and—if available—a list or photos of the major machines you run. We can take it from there.
